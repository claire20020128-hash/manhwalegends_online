## Maximizing Your Gold and Gem Income

Once you have established a solid team, you need to focus on where your resources are coming from. The game offers several "first-time" completion bonuses that should be your absolute priority when you start your journey.

### Strategic Progression Steps
1. **Clear All Story Modes:** Complete every story stage on both Normal and Hard difficulties. These are one-time rewards that provide a massive boost to your starting coffers.
2. **Utilize the Index:** Don't forget to claim your rewards in the Index. Collecting new units yields gems that can be converted into other essential resources.
3. **Guild Participation:** Join a guild as early as possible. Guild shops provide passive boosts, including potential gold and experience multipliers that make long-term grinding much easier.
4. **Challenge Mode:** While not always the most consistent, participating in challenges every 30 minutes can net you additional currency, provided the rotation includes what you need.

## Advanced Strategies for Secret Units

The path to the most powerful units in the game requires significant investment. You cannot simply rely on gold; you must also manage your "constellations" and raid materials carefully.

Player experience suggests that the secret unit tied to the "Ancient" unit is a game-changer. By using Kim to copy the passives of your Ancient unit, you can accelerate the rate at which you collect the 1,850 constellations required for the unlock.

### Raid Preparation Requirements
Raids are the endgame activity for most players. If you want to farm these effectively, ensure your team meets these prerequisites:

* **Level 100 Cap:** Ensure your main damage dealers are at max level.
* **Trait Optimization:** Aim for the "Legend" trait. While it may seem like a luxury, the ability to upgrade units multiple times is critical for raid success.
* **Evolution:** Always unequip your units before attempting an evolution in the workshop to avoid system errors.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the best way to get Manhwa Legends fast gold?**
The most efficient method is grinding the "Hidden Village" map on Hard mode. It offers the best balance of clear time and reward payout compared to other stages.

**Should I focus on farming units or damage units first?**
Always secure your farming units (like Lloyd or Mint) first. Having a stable economy allows you to purchase and upgrade your damage units significantly faster in the long run.

**Why is the "Ancient" unit considered mandatory?**
Ancient is required to farm the constellations needed for one of the meta-defining secret units. Without him, you cannot progress toward that specific unlock.

**How do I get more trait rolls?**
Beyond initial quest rewards, the best ways to obtain trait rolls are through "normalizing" units (which can yield rolls even from rare units) and purchasing them from the guild shop once your guild reaches a sufficient level.
