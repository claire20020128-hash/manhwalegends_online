## Understanding the Manhwa Legends Meta

The current meta in Manhwa Legends revolves around three core pillars: raw Area of Effect (AoE) damage, specialized boss killing, and economic sustainability. Unlike many other tower defense games where you can simply brute-force your way through waves with a single high-DPS unit, this title requires a balanced squad. Player experience suggests that ignoring economy units like Lloyd Master or Mint will lead to a quick defeat in longer game modes where unit costs scale aggressively.

When looking at the current roster, units are categorized not just by their rarity, but by their utility in different game modes. For instance, a unit might be a B-tier DPS but an S-tier support due to crowd control or buffing capabilities. Understanding these nuances is the first step in building a top-tier team.

## The Absolute S-Tier: Units You Must Target

If you are just starting your journey and looking for **Manhwa Legends who to pull**, your primary focus should be the S-tier heavy hitters. These units define the current meta and can carry an account through almost all available content.

### Secret Dreamer: The Current GOAT
Currently regarded as the absolute best unit in the game, Secret Dreamer excels in both DPS and support roles. According to community reports, his full AoE coverage combined with massive damage numbers makes him indispensable. What truly sets him apart is his ability to summon clones that inflict shock-based stuns on enemies. 

However, obtaining him is a process. You first need to pull **Ancient Regressor**, an A-tier DPS in his own right. By investing in Ancient Regressor’s skill tree, he drops "constellations" that players must fuse to unlock the Secret Dreamer. This makes the Ancient Regressor a high-priority pull for anyone aiming for the endgame.

### Suen Vessel: The Boss Specialist
While Secret Dreamer handles the waves, Suen Vessel is the undisputed king of boss encounters. He possesses the highest single-target DPS in the game. When equipped with the "Boss Slayer" trait, he can melt elite enemies in seconds. If you find yourself struggling with high-HP bosses in raids, Suen Vessel should be your top target.

### The DPS Power Trio
Three other units stand at the top of the DPS food chain: White Wolf, Beast Primal, and Bord Mimickry.
*   **White Wolf:** A crit-machine that can stack his critical hit chance up to 100% while applying bleed effects.
*   **Beast Primal:** A consistent performer that remains meta-relevant from the early game all the way to the late game.
*   **Bord Mimickry:** Features a unique passive that allows him to act as a pseudo-full AoE unit, making him incredibly efficient for wave clearing.

## Economy and Support: Building a Balanced Team

A common mistake for new players searching for **Manhwa Legends who to pull** is focusing entirely on damage. In Manhwa Legends, your economy determines how many units you can place and upgrade. Without units like Lloyd Master or Mint, you will run out of "Wand" (the in-game currency) long before the final waves.

### The Economy Duo: Lloyd Master and Mint
These two are the backbone of any successful long-form run. They don't provide much in terms of combat, but their ability to generate currency is unmatched. Players are highly encouraged to roll the "Hustler" trait on these units to maximize their gold production.

### Essential Supports
Support units in Manhwa Legends provide utility that damage alone cannot replace.
*   **Kim Duck Jaw (Immortal King):** While his raw DPS is only B-tier, his support capabilities are S-tier. His time-stop ability provides a massive stun to every enemy in range, and his passive allows him to copy the abilities of other units, making him the most versatile character in the game.
*   **Hyora Idol:** She provides massive damage buffs through her floating nodes. Investing in her skill tree is essential to unlock her full potential.
*   **Violet Grace Revolution:** She is considered essential for boss killing due to her debuffs, though she also provides respectable early-game damage.
*   **Queria Pestilence:** While she functions as an A-tier DPS, she is an S-tier support for boss raids. Her passive damage-over-time effects are critical for taking down high-health targets.

## Trait Synergy and Optimization

Pulling the right unit is only half the battle. To truly optimize your team, you must roll for specific traits that complement the unit's role. Traits are random modifiers that can drastically alter a unit's effectiveness.

| Trait | Best For | Effect Summary |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Legend** | DPS / All-rounders | Significant boost to all stats. |
| **Limit Break** | High-Level DPS | Breaks the standard stat caps for massive power. |
| **Boss Slayer** | Specialized Killers | Increases damage dealt specifically to boss enemies. |
| **Hustler** | Economy Units | Increases the amount of currency generated per wave. |

When deciding **Manhwa Legends who to pull**, you should also consider how much "investment" a unit needs. For example, Infernum Phoenix is a B-tier unit that can become much stronger if paired with other fire-element units, but he is considered "build dependent." If you don't have a fire-themed team, his value drops significantly.

## Early Game Progression and "Bricks" to Avoid

For players just starting out, the initial pulls can be intimidating. If you don't land a top-tier S-rank unit immediately, look for these reliable A and B-tier units to help you progress through the early stages:
*   **Andrasa:** A fantastic support that provides a range and AoE damage buff to your entire team.
*   **Cherry Dancer:** A straightforward, reliable DPS that functions well as a sub-boss killer.
*   **Hyori Princess:** A technical unit that cycles through different damage-over-time (DoT) effects. Once her stacks are maximized, she can shred through enemy armor.

### Units to Avoid (The "Bricks")
Not every legendary character is worth your resources. Based on community testing, **Pildo Gang Landlord** and **Creator** are currently considered C-tier. Pildo uses a turret-based system that sounds interesting on paper but suffers from incredibly low damage output. Creator, on the other hand, has a mechanical kit that is too clunky and awkward to use effectively in high-pressure situations. It is generally advised to save your resources for more impactful units.

## FAQ: Manhwa Legends Who to Pull

### Who is the best overall unit to pull first in Manhwa Legends?
The best overall unit is **Secret Dreamer**. However, since he is a fusion unit, you should actually aim to pull **Ancient Regressor** first. Ancient Regressor is the key to unlocking Secret Dreamer through his skill tree and constellation drops. Once fused, Secret Dreamer provides unparalleled AoE damage and crowd control.

### Are there any limited units worth pulling?
Yes, units like **Boxer** and **J Hope** are limited units that are quite strong for the mid-game. While they may struggle with the scaling in late-game infinite modes compared to S-tier monsters like White Wolf, they are excellent additions to a growing roster and can help you clear difficult story stages.

### Which trait should I look for when pulling for a DPS?
When you are looking at **Manhwa Legends who to pull** for damage, the best traits are **Legend** and **Limit Break**. Legend provides a massive all-around stat boost, while Limit Break allows the unit to surpass standard power ceilings, which is vital for the hardest content in the game.

### Why is Lloyd Master considered a top-tier pull?
Lloyd Master is an economy unit. In Manhwa Legends, the cost of placing and upgrading units increases significantly as the game progresses. Lloyd Master generates extra currency (Wand), allowing you to build a full field of high-powered units that you otherwise wouldn't be able to afford. Pair him with the **Hustler** trait for maximum efficiency.
