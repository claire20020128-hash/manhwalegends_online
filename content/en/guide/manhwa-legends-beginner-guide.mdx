## Understanding the Unit Meta and Summoning Strategy

When you first dive into the game, your initial summons will dictate the pace of your early progression. Not all units are created equal, and knowing which ones to prioritize is essential for efficiency. According to community reports, focusing on versatile units that offer utility alongside damage is the smartest path for newcomers.

### Recommended Early-Game Units

| Unit Name | Role | Why It’s Recommended |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Viol** | Damage | Excellent early-game DPS with range and crit buffs. |
| **Kim** | Utility | Capable of copying any unit's passive abilities. |
| **Ancient** | Support/Requirement | Mandatory for unlocking powerful secret units. |
| **Yori/Lloyd** | Buff/Farm | Essential for damage boosting and resource generation. |

*Player experience suggests* that players should prioritize obtaining Kim early, as his ability to inherit passives allows for creative team compositions that can handle much tougher challenges than a standard damage-dealer team.

## Mastering the Refinement and Trait Systems

Many players new to the game often confuse upgrading with the refinement system. Refinement is how you squeeze maximum power out of your best units. Rather than just leveling up, you use specific stones to refine stats to the next tier.

### Refinement Tier Guidelines

| Tier | Requirement | Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Early** | Basic Stones | Use sparingly to boost core damage stats. |
| **S-Tier** | Advanced Stones | Wait until you have a solid foundation. |
| **Z-Tier** | Secret Stones | Only for your end-game carry units. |

*Note:* You can evolve a unit immediately and refine the stats later. This is a crucial strategy for players who want to unlock higher-tier forms without waiting to farm every single material first.

Regarding traits, the consensus is clear: do not waste them early. Traits are scarce and should be saved for your top-tier mythic units. Treat them as a rare end-game currency rather than something to gamble with during your first few hours of play.

## Navigating Story Mode and Challenges

Progression in Manhwa Legends is split between standard Story Mode and the more challenging Chronicle stages. While Story Mode acts as your primary source of progression, Chronicle stages are where you hunt for specific evolution items.

### Story Mode Progression Overview

1. **Elven Forest:** Focus on core unit leveling and basic trait acquisition.
2. **Hidden Village:** Critical for unlocking specific unit quest lines.
3. **The Bridge:** High-difficulty content for farming advanced materials.

*Community Tip:* If you pull a unit like Viol early, be aware that he requires a specific quest line—reaching wave 60 in the Hidden Village—before he can be fully evolved. Do not get discouraged if your initial team cannot reach this milestone immediately; focus on steady upgrades first.

For more details on game updates and balancing, check out the official [Roblox platform page](https://www.roblox.com) to stay informed on the latest patches.

## Raid Mechanics and Guild Perks

Raids represent the true end-game in Manhwa Legends. Unlike standard stages, raids feature unique mechanics that require manual play rather than reliance on automated macros.

### Essential Raid Strategies

* **Color Matching:** During boss fights, the central podium will glow a specific color. You must interact with the statue that matches that color to prevent the boss from regenerating health.
* **Team Coordination:** Since raids require specific movement and interaction, playing with friends or guild members significantly increases your success rate.
* **Resource Farming:** Raids are the best place to source evolution items and high-end currency for the shop.

Joining a guild is not just for social benefits; it provides tangible progression boosts. As your guild levels up, members unlock perks such as increased shiny encounter rates, movement speed, and reduced shop prices. Aim for a guild that actively participates in these group-wide upgrades.

## Managing the Daily Gold Shop

Your gold shop resets every 24 hours, and it is vital to treat this as a mandatory daily task. You should consistently prioritize purchasing scrolls. Even if you don't need them immediately, having a stockpile will save you from "resource drought" when a new update drops.

### Daily Shop Priority List

| Item Type | Priority | Reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Universal Scrolls** | Very High | Essential for crafting specific element scrolls. |
| **Evolution Items** | High | Prevents farming bottlenecks. |
| **Food/Materials** | Medium | Good for quick level bursts. |
| **Cosmetics** | Low | Purely aesthetic; skip early on. |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the best way to get strong units for beginners?**
Focus your gems on banners featuring high-tier units like Viol or support units like Yori. Always check the index to see which units offer the best passives before you commit your summoning currency.

**Should I use a macro for raids?**
No, community reports strongly advise against it. The raid boss mechanics—specifically the color-matching statues—require manual input. Using a macro often results in interacting with the wrong statue, causing the boss to heal completely.

**Is this Manhwa Legends beginner guide relevant for the full release?**
Yes, the mechanics described here, including the refinement system, guild progression, and the importance of quest-line units, are core components that carry over into the full launch of the game.

**How do I get more traits without wasting them?**
Be patient and save your trait re-roll items. Only use them on your primary mythic units once you have established a stable team that can consistently clear late-game content.
