## Top Tier Units for Beginners and Veterans

While it is tempting to settle for any high-rarity unit, certain characters provide significantly more value during the early and mid-game progression. According to player experience, focusing on units that offer versatility—such as copying passives or massive crit buffs—will save you time and resources in the long run.

One of the standout units is Viol Grace. He is widely considered a premier early-game unit because his active ability doubles his range while granting a 100% critical hit chance and a 40% critical damage boost. This makes him a powerhouse for clearing waves quickly. Another essential unit is Kim Dokja (often referred to as Kim Dolchshire in community circles). His unique strength lies in his ability to copy the passives of any other unit, allowing for experimental and highly potent team combinations.

## The Path to the Secret Unit: Ancient and Constellations

The most prestigious goal for many players is obtaining the "Dreamy" secret unit, the Insect Monarch. However, this isn't a simple summon. To even begin the process, you must obtain and evolve the unit known as Ancient. This character holds a specific "Evo Passive" that allows you to collect a unique resource called Constellations.

Community reports indicate that Constellations are exceptionally rare. You have a roughly 0.4% chance to earn one Constellation upon a standard takedown, though this chance increases to 4% when defeating bosses. To unlock the secret unit, you must accumulate a staggering 1,850 Constellations. Because this is a passive grind, it is highly recommended to evolve Ancient as early as possible so that every match you play contributes toward this long-term goal.

## Refining Stats and Managing Traits

In this **Manhwa Legends guide**, it is crucial to distinguish between "leveling" and "refining." In most Roblox tower defense games, you simply feed units experience to make them stronger. Here, the refining system allows you to sacrifice takedowns to upgrade specific unit attributes, such as their SBA (Special Battle Ability) or Z-tier stats.

Refining isn't guaranteed; it operates on a percentage-based success system. For example, moving a unit's SBA to the maximum tier might only have a 40% success rate per attempt. If you fail, you lose the takedowns but keep the unit. This system is praised by the community because it allows players to evolve a unit immediately and worry about perfecting their stats later.

Regarding traits, the general consensus is to be extremely conservative with your rerolls. Traits are scarce and hard to come by, similar to the system found in the popular [Roblox anime games](https://www.roblox.com/) community. You do not necessarily need a 0.1% Mythic trait to clear the game’s content. Many players have successfully completed the hardest stages using only Legendary traits on their Mythic Evolved units. Save your trait crystals for your absolute best units and avoid the temptation to gamble them away on mid-tier characters.

## Evolution Mechanics and Scroll Crafting

Evolution is the primary way to unlock a unit's true potential and their special passives. To evolve a unit, you will need specific scrolls ranging from D-grade to S-grade. These function similarly to essences or stars in other games. While you can earn these through challenges, the most consistent way to obtain them is through the crafting system and the daily shop.

Every daily challenge rewards you with a "Universal Scroll" (or blank scroll). These can be used to craft specific elemental scrolls required for different units. If you are preparing for a future update, farming these scrolls in advance is a smart strategy to ensure you can evolve new units the moment they drop.

| Scroll Tier | Acquisition Method | Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **D to B Grade** | Common Challenges / Shop | Early-stage evolutions |
| **A to S Grade** | Hard Challenges / Crafting | Mythic and Secret evolutions |
| **Elemental Scrolls** | Universal Scroll Crafting | Specific unit requirements (Light, Dark, etc.) |
| **Universal Scroll** | Daily Challenges | Crafting base for all other scrolls |

## Mastering Raids and the Statue Mechanic

Raids represent the true endgame of Manhwa Legends. Unlike story mode, where you can often rely on raw power, raids introduce interactive mechanics that can easily lead to a wipe if ignored. The most famous mechanic involves the Temple statues.

During a raid, the central podium will light up with a specific color. Players must then identify the statue matching that color and "pray" at it. If the team fails to interact with the correct statue, or interacts with the wrong one, the raid boss will regenerate all of his health. This mechanic makes macroing or "AFK-ing" raids nearly impossible, as it requires active participation and coordination. 

The drop rates in raids have recently been improved. Previously, certain high-tier items had a 1% drop rate, but recent updates have increased this to 2% with a 50% pity drop rate, making the endgame grind significantly more rewarding for dedicated players.

## Guild Benefits and Social Progression

Joining a guild is one of the most beneficial things you can do in this game. You must reach Level 10 to create or join a guild, but the perks are well worth the effort. Guilds allow players to pool their resources into a "Vault," which is then used to buy permanent boosts for all members.

As your guild levels up, you unlock various perks that make the game easier. At level 10, you can expand the member cap up to 60 players. By the time a guild reaches level 100, members enjoy significant boosts to shiny odds, movement speed, and even cheaper prices in the gold shop.

## Story Mode and Chronicle Stages

The story mode is divided into three primary worlds: the Elven Forest, the Hidden Village, and the Bridge. Each world consists of five acts followed by an Infinite mode. Unlike the standard six-act structure seen in other games, the five-act structure keeps the pacing brisk while offering plenty of opportunities for rewards.

For players looking to evolve their units, the Chronicle stages (also known as Legend stages) are essential. These stages are the primary source for evolution items. For example, Act 2 might drop a specific sword needed for a warrior unit, while Act 3 drops strands for a mage-type character. If you have a specific unit you want to evolve, check the Chronicle rewards list first to see where you need to spend your stamina.

Additionally, some units are tied to specific questlines. For instance, obtaining Viol Grace's evolution item requires you to have him on your team and reach Wave 60 in the Hidden Village Infinite mode. This "Blue Parasite" questline is a common hurdle for new players, so ensure your team is sufficiently leveled before attempting it.

## FAQ: Manhwa Legends Guide

### What is the fastest way to get the Secret Unit in Manhwa Legends?
The fastest way is to evolve the Ancient unit as soon as possible. Once evolved, his passive allows you to collect Constellations from takedowns. Focus on farming bosses, as they have a 4% drop rate compared to the 0.4% rate for normal enemies. You will need 1,850 Constellations in total.

### How does the refining system work?
Refining allows you to upgrade a unit's specific stats (like SBA or Z-stats) by sacrificing a certain number of takedowns. It is a percentage-based system, meaning you might need multiple attempts to reach the maximum stat tier. This **Manhwa Legends guide** recommends refining your units only after you have evolved them to their final form.

### Are traits important for clearing the endgame?
While Mythic traits are powerful, they are not strictly necessary. The game is balanced well enough that players can clear most content, including raids and Infinite wave 60, using Legendary traits. Because trait crystals are scarce, you should only use them on top-tier Mythic or Secret units.

### What happens if I click the wrong statue in a raid?
In raids, if you or your team prays at the wrong colored statue, the boss will immediately regenerate all of its health. This mechanic ensures that players must remain active and observant during the fight, preventing the use of simple macros to clear endgame content.
