Specifically, completing both Normal and Hard modes for every stage is mandatory. According to experienced players, the **Hidden Village** map is the absolute best location for repetitive farming. ## The Evolution and Crafting System

Simply owning a Mythic unit isn't enough to tackle the end-game. Evolution is required to unlock the full potential of your roster. Unlike some games where you just need duplicates, Manhwa Legends utilizes a workshop and quest-based system.

For example, evolving Lloyd requires you to visit the Workshop and craft a "Developer's Shovel." Other units, like Violet Grace, require you to complete a special quest involving 60 stages of Infinite Mode and the sacrifice of another high-tier unit. This system adds a layer of strategy to how you spend your **Manhwa Legends fast gems**, as you may need to pull for "sacrifice fodder" to empower your main team.

To track your progress and see which materials are currently available, you can visit the [official Roblox game page](https://www.roblox.com/games/) to check for the latest patch notes and developer updates regarding drop rates.

## Optimizing Stats and Traits

Once your units are evolved, the next step in the power-scaling process is the Stat and Trait system. This is often where players feel the most frustration due to the RNG (random number generation) involved.

### The Stat Refining Process
To improve a unit's stats, you must use Crystals, which can be obtained via the Guild Shop, Gold Shop, or by crafting them in the Workshop. After refining a stat, you must then spend "Take Downs" (kills accumulated by that unit) to enhance those stats further. It is a dual-layered system that rewards both active play and resource management.

### Choosing the Right Trait
Traits can completely change how a unit performs. While many players hunt for damage-oriented traits, the **Legend** trait is currently considered the best for most high-cost units.
*   **Why Legend?** Units like Ancient can be upgraded multiple times, with the ability to "restart" the upgrade path to stack even more damage. This process is incredibly expensive. The Legend trait reduces costs and increases efficiency, making it almost mandatory for units with complex upgrade paths.
*   **A Warning:** Avoid putting the Legend trait on your farm units (Lloyd/Mint). Since their primary job is to make money, you want traits that enhance their utility or specialized stats rather than their combat cost-efficiency.

## Guild Benefits and Long-term Growth

If you are struggling to find **Manhwa Legends fast gems**, the guild shop serves as one of the few places where you can consistently acquire Treasure Rolls once the initial story achievements are exhausted.

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

As the game is still in its early stages, there are several "quality of life" issues that players should be aware of:
*   **Auto-Upgrade Bug:** The current auto-upgrade system is difficult to toggle off once started. Furthermore, the order in which you click your units sets their priority for upgrades. Always click your most important damage dealers first.
*   **Macroing Raids:** Raid Act 3 features randomized mechanics that make macroing (using automated scripts) almost impossible. You will need to play these stages manually to ensure you hit the 50-clear pity.
*   **Shiny Normalizing:** If you have extra shiny units, you can "normalize" them to receive Treasure Rolls. This is one of the best ways to recycle unwanted luck into useful resources.

## FAQ: Manhwa Legends Strategy

### What is the fastest way to get Manhwa Legends fast gems?
The most efficient method is farming **Story Mode on Hard Difficulty**, specifically the **Hidden Village** map. This map is shorter than others, allowing for faster completions and a consistent reward of 110 gems per run.

### Which unit should I evolve first?
Most players recommend evolving **Lloyd** first if you have him, as he is the premier farm unit. However, for damage, **Ancient** is a top priority because his evolution and unique mechanic allow you to farm for Secret units.

### How do I get more Treasure Rolls after finishing the story?
Once story rewards are claimed, Treasure Rolls become scarce. You can obtain them by **normalizing shiny units**, purchasing them from a high-level **Guild Shop**, or completing the Battle Pass and daily achievements.

### Is the "Legend" trait worth keeping?
Yes, especially on units like **Ancient** that have multiple upgrade tiers. The Legend trait helps manage the high costs of maxing out a unit's potential during a match. Do not, however, use it on farm units like Mint or Lloyd.
