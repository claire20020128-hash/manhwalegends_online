## D and C Tier: The Progression Phase
When you first start your journey, you might see traits like Rogue 1 or Fighter 1 and think they provide a nice boost. According to recent community reports, these are strictly temporary. They are fine for your very first few levels, but as you approach the mid-game, they become dead weight.

### Why You Should Replace Low-Tier Traits
* **Inefficiency:** The stat increases are too small to impact higher-level enemy health pools.
* **Resource Management:** Don't waste your reroll currency trying to upgrade a D-tier trait; wait until you have enough to aim for at least a B or A-tier result.
* **Scaling:** As units level up, percentage-based buffs from higher tiers scale significantly better than the flat bonuses found in the bottom tiers.

## B Tier: The Reliable Workhorses
Once you enter the mid-game, you need traits that offer consistent value. The B-tier options—such as Sunderer and Marksman 3—are the bread and butter of a functional roster. These traits don't necessarily break the game, but they provide the stability required to farm materials efficiently.

Player experience suggests that Sunderer is particularly effective for units that already have high base damage, as it provides a multiplicative impact against tougher bosses. Similarly, if you find yourself struggling with enemy positioning, moving up to a Ranger or Marksman 3 trait can provide that extra breathing room to keep your squishier units safe.

## A Tier: Specialized Performance
A-tier traits are where things get interesting. These are not just "stat sticks"; they are strategic choices that define your team's purpose. If you are building a dedicated boss-killing squad, for instance, you should be hunting for the Boss Slayer trait.

### When to Prioritize A-Tier Traits
* **Boss Slayer:** Essential for high-HP raids where single-target DPS is the only metric that matters.
* **Swiftness:** Perfect for units with high base attack speed; stacking this can lead to massive DPS spikes.
* **Hustler:** The go-to for farming units. If you are looking to optimize your resource generation, this is your best friend.
* **Exposure:** A utility-focused choice that helps your entire team maximize their damage potential against debuffed targets.

## S Tier: The Elite Meta
If you are aiming for the top of the leaderboards, you need S-tier traits. These rolls don't just add a percentage; they fundamentally change how a unit functions. The current meta is dominated by four specific traits that every top-tier player is hunting for.

### The Elite Four
1. **Legend:** The gold standard for raw DPS. It provides a massive, flat damage increase that makes any carry unit significantly more dangerous.
2. **Ruler:** A unique trait that changes your unit's elemental identity, allowing you to bypass elemental weaknesses or exploit specific boss vulnerabilities.
3. **Limit Break:** The ultimate all-rounder. It improves damage, attack speed, and range simultaneously, making it the most versatile trait in the game.
4. **Assassin:** If your unit has a high critical hit rate, this is non-negotiable. It scales your crits to absurd levels, perfect for glass-cannon builds.

| Trait | Primary Stat Boost | Best Unit Archetype |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Legend** | Raw Damage | Main DPS / Carries |
| **Ruler** | Elemental | Situational / Elemental Counters |
| **Limit Break** | All Stats | All-arounders / Mythics |
| **Assassin** | Crit Damage/Rate | High-Crit Assassins / Snipers |

## Tips for Effective Rerolling
Rerolling is a gamble, but you can tilt the odds in your favor. First, always prioritize your main DPS unit. A perfect trait on a sub-optimal unit is a waste of currency. Second, check out the [official Manhwa Legends Discord](https://www.roblox.com/games/123456789/Manhwa-Legends) to see if there are active codes that provide free reroll tokens.

Don't fall into the trap of chasing an S-tier trait for every single member of your team. It is often better to have a full team of A-tier traits than to have one "perfect" unit and four units with D-tier placeholders. Balance your roster, focus on your primary carry first, and always keep an eye on upcoming patch notes, as the meta shifts rapidly with new unit releases.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which is the best trait for a main DPS unit?
According to our **Manhwa Legends traits tier list**, Legend is widely considered the best choice for a primary damage dealer due to its massive raw damage boost. However, if your unit relies heavily on critical hits, Assassin is an equally devastating alternative.

### Should I keep a B-tier trait while rerolling?
Yes. B-tier traits like Sunderer are perfectly capable of carrying you through most content. Only start aggressively rerolling for S-tier traits once your entire team is at least at the B-tier level, as your currency is better spent elsewhere early on.

### Does the Ruler trait change a unit's element permanently?
The Ruler trait modifies a unit's elemental identity, which is incredibly powerful for optimizing against specific boss weaknesses. It is a highly sought-after trait for players who want to build specialized teams for endgame raids.

### How often does the trait meta change?
The meta in Manhwa Legends can shift whenever a new update introduces a new character, balance pass, or game mechanic. We recommend checking back here regularly to see how new units affect the current rankings and to ensure your roster stays competitive.
