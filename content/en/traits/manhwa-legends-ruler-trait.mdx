## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the Manhwa Legends Ruler trait better than Legend?
It depends on your team composition. The Legend trait provides a higher raw damage boost, making it better for general-purpose DPS. However, the **Manhwa Legends Ruler trait** is superior for elemental-specific builds and overcoming elemental resistances in late-game stages.

### How do I get the Manhwa Legends Ruler trait?
You can obtain the Ruler trait by using reroll tokens on your units. It is an S-tier trait, meaning it has a very low drop rate compared to common traits like Fighter or Rogue. It is recommended to save your rerolls for your most powerful characters.

### Does the Ruler trait affect all units the same way?
No. The **Manhwa Legends Ruler trait** is most effective on units that already have strong base stats but are limited by their elemental type. Putting Ruler on a support unit is generally considered a waste of a high-tier roll, as support units usually benefit more from utility traits like Exposure or Swiftness.
