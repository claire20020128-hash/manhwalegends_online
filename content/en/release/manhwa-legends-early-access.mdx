## The Innovative Skill Tree System

One of the most praised features during the Manhwa Legends early access period is the unit skill tree. Moving away from the standard "level up for stats" model, this game introduces a branching tree that dictates how a unit functions on the battlefield. Every unit automatically unlocks a character-specific passive upon acquisition, but the true power is found in the higher levels.

According to community reports, the skill tree serves as the backbone of unit customization. Players must level their units to specific milestones to unlock "Notable Passives." These passives can range from simple stat boosts to complex mechanics like bleed damage or critical strike chance increases.

### Skill Tree Progression Milestones

| Level Milestone | Unlockable | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Level 1 | Innate Passive | The base ability all units start with upon summoning. |
| Level 10 | First Node | Usually a minor buff to range, damage, or attack speed. |
| Level 50 | Second Notable | A significant ability that often changes the unit's utility. |
| Level 100 | Final Passive | The ultimate buff, often providing massive scaling bonuses. |
| Evolution | Innate Node | A special passive unlocked only after the unit is evolved. |

For example, the "White Wolf" unit—a popular Mythic—features a passive called "Blood Hunt." When this unit lands a critical strike, it inflicts a 20% bleed effect based on the unit's total damage. At level 100, this scaling increases further, granting 2.5% extra crit damage for every bleeding enemy within its range. This synergy encourages players to build teams that capitalize on specific status effects.

## Attributes and the Ore Refining System

Beyond leveling and skill trees, the game introduces a complex "Attribute" system. This is where the game separates the casual players from the hardcore strategists. Attributes are essentially sub-stats that can be refined and advanced using various ores found throughout the game.

The refining process is not guaranteed. Each ore has a specific "Success Chance," meaning players must weigh the risks of attempting an upgrade. Once an attribute is successfully refined, it can be "Advanced," further pushing the unit's power ceiling. This layer of RNG adds a long-term goal for players who have already maxed out their unit levels.

### Common Traits and Their Effects

Traits are another layer of customization applied to units, often rerolled using specific in-game currency. During the Manhwa Legends early access phase, several high-tier traits have been identified by the community:

*   **Monarch:** The pinnacle of traits, featuring a 1,300-pull pity system. It provides massive across-the-board buffs.
*   **Ruler:** Offers a 20% range increase and a discount on SBA (Special Battle Ability) costs. It also changes the unit's element to Angelic.
*   **Limit Break:** A high-risk, high-reward trait that increases placement cost by 25% but offers a substantial damage multiplier.
*   **Swiftness:** Every fourth attack hits twice, and it provides a 10% boost to SBA generation.
*   **Wealth:** A specialized trait for farm units, increasing money earned from takedowns and waves by 20%.

## Actionable Tips for Early Access Players

1.  **Prioritize the Skill Tree:** Don't just look at base damage. A unit with a lower base stat but a powerful level 50 passive may outperform a raw power unit in the long run.
2.  **Save Your Ores:** Don't waste high-success-rate ores on Rare or Epic units. Save them for your Mythics and Secrets to ensure you don't run out of materials when it matters most.
3.  **Utilize Buffers and Healers:** The game features dedicated support units that can buff range and damage or even cleanse debuffs. In later story acts, these units are mandatory for survival.
4.  **Check Level Milestones:** Always keep an eye on the level milestones menu. Knowing exactly when your unit will get its next big power spike helps you decide which units to bring into difficult raids.
5.  **Farm Gold Early:** Use units with the "Wealth" trait to maximize your gold income during easier story stages. This gold is essential for the attribute refining system.

## FAQ: Manhwa Legends Early Access

### How do I get into Manhwa Legends early access?
Entry is typically managed through developer-led programs for content creators or specific testers. However, the game often opens up "Early Access Plus" periods for players who purchase specific game passes or join the official community groups. Keep an eye on social media for announcement codes.

### What is the best unit to summon for in the current version?
Currently, the "White Wolf" Mythic unit is highly recommended due to its bleed-stacking passive and excellent scaling at level 100. Additionally, looking for units with the Monarch or Ruler traits will give you a significant advantage in both story mode and raids.

### Is the skill tree system permanent for all units?
Yes, every unit you summon has its own individual skill tree. Once you unlock a node, it stays with that unit. If you evolve the unit, you unlock a special "Innate Evolution Node" that provides an even greater power boost.

### How does the attribute refining system work?
You select a unit and a specific stat (like damage or range) and then use ores to attempt a refine. Each ore has a different success percentage. If successful, the stat increases, and you can eventually "Advance" the attribute to reach even higher tiers of power. For the best results in Manhwa Legends early access, always try to use the highest quality ores on your primary damage dealers.
