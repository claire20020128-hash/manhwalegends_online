## The Hype and The Harsh Reality of the Launch

When the **Manhwa Legends release date** finally arrived on Roblox, the gaming community was braced for a massive shakeup in the tower defense genre. Expectations were astronomically high, as players had spent over two years waiting for a project that promised to bring popular webcomic aesthetics to the tower defense format. Unfortunately, the **Manhwa Legends release date** is now often cited as one of the most significant cautionary tales in Roblox history.

Within mere hours of its launch, the game saw its concurrent player count crater from a staggering 70,000 users to just a few thousand. For many, the transition from anticipation to disappointment was almost instantaneous. While the developers invested heavily in marketing, the final product struggled to match the quality of established titans like *Anime Adventures* or *Anime Expeditions*.

## Gameplay Mechanics and The "Lack of Depth" Issue

One of the most persistent critiques regarding the post-launch state of the game involves the fundamental lack of gameplay depth. Tower defense enthusiasts rely on specific mechanics to strategize, such as "pre-farming" (placing economic units before the first wave) or "phantom placement." 

The game’s inability to support these standard features made the combat feel sluggish. Furthermore, the character roster was a major point of confusion for the community. Instead of prioritizing the main characters that fans of specific webcomics know and love, the developers opted for background figures. This decision alienated the very audience that was most excited for the **Manhwa Legends release date**.

### Technical Issues and Security Risks
Beyond the gameplay flaws, the game was plagued by an unusual security incident. During the initial release window, a malicious actor managed to create a fake version of the game by renaming an unrelated project. Because this fake version stayed up for a significant duration, many players unknowingly spent money on an illegitimate product. This "exit scam" incident, while technically not the direct fault of the developers, added a layer of toxicity to the game's already difficult launch.
