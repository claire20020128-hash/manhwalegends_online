## How to Obtain the Manhwa Legends Beylord Unit

To get your hands on **Manhwa Legends Beylord**, you will need to keep a close eye on the rotating banner. The game features a "Banner" system where specific units have increased drop rates for a limited time. 

According to player experience, using Luck Potions is highly recommended when Beylord appears in the rotation. There are two types of potions:
1. **Regular Luck Potion:** Provides a modest boost to higher rarity drops.
2. **Super Luck Potion:** Significantly increases the chances of pulling Legendaries and Mythics.

If you are struggling to pull the unit naturally, the pity system ensures that you will eventually receive a high-rarity unit. However, since Beylord is often a Legendary or higher tier depending on the specific variant, you may need to save up to 5,000–10,000 Gems to guarantee a successful session.

### Farming Gems for Summons

For F2P (Free to Play) players, the best way to accumulate the Gems needed for a **Manhwa Legends Beylord** pull is through the following methods:
*   **Story Mode:** Completing new acts for the first time provides a massive influx of Gems.
*   **AFK Chamber:** If you cannot actively play, leaving your character in the AFK chamber overnight is a reliable way to slowly build your Gem reserves.
*   **Daily Challenges:** These often reward scrolls and gems, which are vital for the crafting and summoning loop.

The primary reason veteran players hunt for the **Manhwa Legends Beylord** unit is to evolve **Violet Grace** (the game's version of Bam from *Tower of God*). Violet Grace is one of the most versatile units in the game, but his base form lacks the punch needed for late-game Infinite Mode.

To evolve Violet Grace into his "25th Bam" or "Slayer Candidate" forms, the evolution menu typically requires a specific recipe. Community reports indicate that the recipe often looks like this:

| Requirement Type | Item/Unit Needed | Quantity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| Sacrifice Unit | **Manhwa Legends Beylord** | 1 |

The "Parasite" items are usually obtained by reaching specific waves in high-difficulty maps, such as the Hidden Sand Village. Reaching wave 60 or 61 is often the benchmark for unlocking these rare crafting materials.

## Optimizing Your Team: Traits and Stats

Once you have secured your units, the next step is rolling for Traits. A unit’s effectiveness can be tripled simply by having the right Trait. In Manhwa Legends, Traits are rolled using Reroll Tokens, which can be obtained through codes or the Gem shop.

### Top Tier Traits for Units

| Trait Name | Effect | Best For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hustler** | Increases Yen/Money generation. | Farm units (like Lloyd). |
| **Limit Break** | Massive 50% Damage boost, but increases cost. | Primary DPS. |
| **Ruler** | Increases range and provides a slight damage buff. | AoE (Area of Effect) units. |
| **Fighter** | Standard damage increase (Levels 1-3). | General attackers. |
| **Godly** | The rarest trait; provides massive buffs to all stats. | Any Mythic or Secret unit. |

When managing your **Manhwa Legends Beylord** or the units you evolve using it, pay attention to their base stats (SBA, Range, and Damage). You can upgrade these stats using "Takedowns" (XP earned by the unit during gameplay). Aim for at least "A" or "S" rank stats to ensure your team can handle the scaling health of enemies in Act 5 and beyond.

## Advanced Gameplay Tips for Manhwa Legends

Navigating the early access phase of a game like this can be tricky. Here are some actionable tips based on current player trends:

1.  **Don't Ignore Farm Units:** Units like Lloyd or Pildo Gang (Landlord version) are essential. Without a steady stream of Yen during a match, you won't be able to afford the expensive final upgrades for your Mythics.
2.  **Stack Luck Potions:** If you have multiple luck potions, check if they stack. Usually, a Super Luck and a Regular Luck potion can be active at the same time, giving you the best possible odds for a **Manhwa Legends Beylord** pull.
3.  **Check for Codes:** The developers frequently release codes for "Early Access" or "Release Delays." These codes often provide 100+ rerolls, which are worth thousands of Robux.
4.  **Positioning Matters:** Use the "Show Max Range" toggle in the settings. Placing your AoE units at corners or "U-turns" on the map maximizes their damage uptime.

## FAQ

### How do I get the Manhwa Legends Beylord unit?
You can obtain the **Manhwa Legends Beylord** unit primarily through the summoning banner using Gems. It is often a high-rarity unit, so using Luck Potions during its featured rotation is the most effective strategy.

### Why is Beylord required for evolution?
In Manhwa Legends, certain Mythic units like Violet Grace require a "sacrifice" of specific Legendary units to unlock their final forms. The **Manhwa Legends Beylord** unit is one of the most common requirements for these high-tier evolutions.

### Can I evolve Beylord himself?
Currently, the focus for the **Manhwa Legends Beylord** unit is its role as a sacrifice for other evolutions. However, the game is frequently updated, and future patches may introduce an evolved "High Ranker" version of the Beylord family.

### What are the best traits for a team using Beylord?
If you are using units evolved via **Manhwa Legends Beylord**, aim for traits like **Limit Break** for pure damage or **Ruler** for increased range. If you are using a support unit like Lloyd, the **Hustler** trait is best for maximizing money generation.
