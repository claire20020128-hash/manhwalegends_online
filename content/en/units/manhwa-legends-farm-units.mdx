## Advanced Strategies: Stats and Traits

The stat system in Manhwa Legends can be polarizing, but it is a necessary evil if you want to tackle high-difficulty raids. Refinement requires significant dedication and resource management.

### Understanding the Stat Refinement Process
* **Refining:** Use crystals gathered from challenges or gold shops to reroll your base stats.
* **Enhancing:** Once you have a desirable stat distribution, spend your kill counts to enhance those values.
* **Trait Rolling:** Aim for the "Legend" trait on every unit. Even if other traits offer better theoretical damage, the cost-to-upgrade reduction provided by the Legend trait is vital for maxing out units like Ancient, which require multiple upgrades to reach their peak.

Avoid rolling for high-tier traits on your farming units. It is a common player mistake to waste precious trait rolls on a unit like Mint or Lloyd when that currency should be dedicated to your primary damage dealers.

## Raid Mechanics and Secret Unit Acquisition

Raids represent the endgame of Manhwa Legends. With a 50-pity system and high-difficulty requirements, preparation is critical. Player experience warns that trying to run these without level 100 units and optimal traits will result in failure.

### Secret Unit Unlock Methods
* **Banner Summoning:** A 0.005% drop rate makes this a pure gamble, though pity systems are suspected to exist.
* **Constellation Exchange:** Use the currency farmed by your Ancient unit to redeem the meta secret unit.
* **Raid Drops:** High-damage units, specifically those involving "Sanji's dad," are exclusive to the challenging Raid Act 3.

Because macroing is currently disabled in Raid Act 3 due to specific anti-automation mechanics, you must manually participate to grind these rewards. It is recommended to save your strongest units exclusively for these encounters.
