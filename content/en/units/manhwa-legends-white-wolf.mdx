## Combat Performance and Strategic Placement

Placement is the difference between a victory and a crushing defeat. The **Manhwa Legends White Wolf** is a ground-based unit, meaning it must be placed adjacent to the path. However, its range allows for some flexibility.

### Optimal Placement Zones

*   **Corner Pockets:** Placing the White Wolf at a U-turn or corner maximizes its time-on-target. Because of its high attack speed, it can land dozens of hits as the enemy rounds the bend.
*   **Early Path Buffing:** If you are using support units that provide damage buffs, the White Wolf should be within their aura. Its fast attack speed means it benefits more from flat damage buffs than slower, heavier hitters.
*   **Chokepoints:** Use this unit near the end of a chokepoint to "clean up" any fast-moving enemies that survived your initial AOE (Area of Effect) blasts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I get the Manhwa Legends White Wolf unit?
The White Wolf can be obtained through the game's summoning system using gems or by completing specific high-level challenges during limited-time events. Community reports suggest that the drop rate for this legendary unit is approximately 2%, so saving your summons for a "Rate Up" banner is highly recommended.

### Is the Fire Evolution permanent for the White Wolf?
Yes, once you evolve the **Manhwa Legends White Wolf** into its fire form, the change is permanent for that specific unit instance. However, you can still obtain and maintain a standard version of the unit if you wish to have both types in your inventory for different strategic needs.

### Which maps are best for the White Wolf unit?
The White Wolf excels on maps with long straightaways or multiple tight turns. Maps like "Forgotten Field" or "Arena of Legends" provide the ideal terrain for maximizing its range and attack speed. Avoid maps with extremely short paths unless you have enough support units to slow down the enemies significantly.
