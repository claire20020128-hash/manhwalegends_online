## Evolution and Crafting Strategies
The evolution system in Manhwa Legends is complex and requires specific materials, such as scrolls and primal strands. Player experience suggests that jumping into legend-tier stages without being prepared is a recipe for failure. Instead, focus on these three pillars of growth:

1. **Daily Challenges:** These are your primary source for essential crafting materials. Never skip a day.
2. **Shiny Normalization:** Use this feature to turn your lower-tier shiny units into useful resources rather than letting them clutter your inventory.
3. **Passive Rerolling:** Don't waste all your ores on a single unit. Focus on securing a solid "Boss Slayer" or "Crit" trait on your main carry before moving to support units.

## Combat Mechanics: The "Bleed" and Stun Meta
One of the most discussed topics in the community is the "bleed" mechanic. If you manage to stack bleed effectively, you can down enemies with millions of health. However, because the game often lacks auto-upgrade or macro features, players must be highly active during matches to ensure their units are upgraded as soon as funds become available.

### Essential Combat Tips
*   **Don't Over-place:** It is better to have three max-leveled units than six low-level ones.
*   **Stall Tactics:** Use units with stun abilities early in the lane to bunch up enemies for area-of-effect attacks.
*   **Know Your Role:** If your unit is a "Farmer," prioritize its placement in the first wave to boost your income for the rest of the match.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What makes the Manhwa Legends Creator unit so important?
The Creator unit is vital because it provides a percentage-based damage boost to your entire party based on their collective levels. It acts as a passive engine that makes every other unit in your setup perform significantly better.

### How do I get more Gems for summons?
You can earn Gems by completing story chapters on both normal and hard difficulties, finishing daily challenges, and reaching high waves in infinite mode. Some players also look for active codes to boost their starting pool.

### Is it better to focus on Mythic units or evolving Legendaries?
Generally, you should secure a reliable Mythic unit to carry your DPS, but evolving Legendaries is necessary for late-game content. Evolving units often unlocks powerful new passives that can outclass unevolved Mythics.

### Why do my units stop doing damage in higher waves?
This usually happens because enemies in higher waves have extreme damage reduction or high health pools. Ensure your units have the right traits—specifically "Boss Slayer" for tougher waves—and that you are using units that can apply armor-shredding status effects.
