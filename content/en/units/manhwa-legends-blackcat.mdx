## Frequently Asked Questions

### What accessories come with this action figure?
The figure comes packaged with three key accessories: a pair of alternate swappable hands (perfect for open-clawed or gripping poses) and the legendary Wand of Watoomb artifact.

### Is the Manhwa Legends Blackcat suitable for younger children?
While the packaging states it is safe for ages 4 and up, the **Manhwa Legends Blackcat** is primarily designed as a highly detailed collectible for older kids, teens, and adult collectors due to small parts that present a choking hazard.

### How tall is the figure and does it fit with other toy lines?
The figure stands at approximately 5.9 inches (15 cm) tall, which is the standard 6-inch scale. It fits perfectly alongside other Marvel Legends, GI Joe Classified, and standard import action figures.

### Can I swap the head with older Black Cat figures?
Yes, the head can be swapped with older releases. However, community reports indicate that you may need a small amount of adhesive putty or sticky tac to ensure a secure fit on older neck pegs.
