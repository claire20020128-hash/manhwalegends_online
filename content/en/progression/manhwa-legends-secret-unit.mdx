## The Constellation Grind: Using Ancient to Your Advantage

One of the most unique aspects of the game is the constellation system. To earn a **Manhwa Legends secret unit** through this method, you cannot simply rely on any damage dealer. You must utilize a specific unit known as "Ancient." Player experience highlights Ancient as the most important early-game investment because he is the only unit capable of generating constellations upon killing enemies.

When Ancient secures a kill, there is a chance for a constellation to drop. You need to accumulate 1,850 of these to exchange for the secret unit. Because this unit is meta-defining, the earlier you start farming with Ancient, the sooner you can pivot your team toward endgame content.

### Optimizing Constellation Farming

To speed up this process, veteran players recommend a specific synergy involving "Kim." Kim is a support unit capable of copying passives. By using Kim to mimic Ancient’s abilities, you effectively double your chances of generating constellations during a match. This strategy is vital for players who want to bypass the long grind and unlock their **Manhwa Legends secret unit** as quickly as possible.

## Mastering the Raid: The Path to the Highest Damage Unit

If you are looking for the absolute highest damage output in the game, you must look toward Raid Act 3. This stage is notoriously difficult and features a secret unit modeled after a legendary anime father figure. Community reports indicate that this unit is currently the strongest damage dealer available, but obtaining him is a test of endurance.

The raid features a pity system set at 50 runs. Given that each run can take approximately eight minutes and involves randomized mechanics that prevent easy macro usage, players must be prepared for a significant time investment. It is highly recommended to have your units at level 100 and fully evolved before attempting to farm this **Manhwa Legends secret unit**.

### Raid Preparation Checklist

*   **Level Cap:** Ensure your main DPS units are Level 100.
*   **Traits:** Aim for the "Legend" trait to reduce upgrade costs during the raid.
*   **Team Composition:** Bring a mix of stunners (like Violet Grace) and time-stoppers (like Kim).
*   **Guild Perks:** Join a high-level guild to benefit from damage and gold boosts.

## Progression Strategy: Gems, Gold, and Trait Rolls

To even stand a chance at unlocking a **Manhwa Legends secret unit**, you need a steady flow of resources. The game offers several ways to farm gems and gold, but efficiency is key. The fastest way to accumulate wealth is by completing Story Mode on both Normal and Hard difficulties.

Hard mode stages, particularly those in the "Hidden Village" map, are favored by the community. Hidden Village is known for having a shorter path, allowing for faster clear times. Each Hard mode run provides roughly 110 gems, which is significantly higher than the rewards from Chronicle or Challenge stages.

## The Stat and Trait System: Min-Maxing Your Units

Manhwa Legends introduces a stat system that differs from traditional tower defense games. Instead of static growth, you must use crystals to refine your units and then spend "take downs" (kills) to enhance those refined stats. While some players find this system tedious, it is the only way to ensure your units have the raw power necessary for Raid Act 3.

When it comes to traits, the "Legend" trait is widely considered the best for most units. This is because the cost of fully upgrading units like Ancient—who can be reset and upgraded four times for massive damage boosts—is incredibly high. The Legend trait provides the economic relief needed to reach maximum potential in a single match. However, you should avoid placing this trait on dedicated farming units like Mint or Lloyd, as their primary purpose is utility rather than raw damage scaling.

### Trait Priority Ranking

1.  **Legend:** Top tier for expensive Mythic and Secret units.
2.  **Godly:** Excellent for raw damage output.
3.  **Speedy:** Useful for support units like Kim to trigger abilities faster.
4.  **Range:** Critical for units like Violet Grace to maximize stun coverage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I get a Manhwa Legends secret unit faster?
The most reliable way to get a secret unit quickly is to use the Ancient unit paired with Kim. Ancient drops constellations on kills, and Kim can copy this passive, effectively doubling your currency gain. Additionally, farming the Hidden Village map on Hard mode will give you the gems needed for banner pulls.

### Is the Raid Act 3 secret unit worth the 50 pity?
Yes, the Raid Act 3 unit is currently reported to be the highest damage dealer in the game. While the 50-run pity is a significant time commitment, the unit's ability to clear endgame content makes it a necessary addition for any competitive player.

### Which trait should I aim for on my secret units?
The "Legend" trait is generally the best choice for a **Manhwa Legends secret unit**. These units often have very high upgrade costs, and the Legend trait helps mitigate those costs, allowing you to reach their full damage potential much earlier in a match.

### Can I farm Trait Rolls consistently?
Consistent Trait Roll farming is difficult. The best methods include completing all one-time achievements, participating in challenges (though they only reset every 30 minutes), and purchasing them from the Guild Shop once your guild reaches a high enough level. You can also obtain them through "shiny normalizing" by converting shiny units you don't need.
