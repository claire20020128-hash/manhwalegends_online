## What is Manhwa Legends?

Manhwa Legends is a sophisticated Roblox tower defense (TD) game that tasks players with summoning powerful units to defend their base against waves of increasingly difficult enemies. The core gameplay loop involves gathering currency, summoning characters from a gacha-style system, and strategically placing them on a variety of maps. What sets this game apart is its thematic focus on manhwa—a term for Korean comics—and manhua, their Chinese counterparts.

The game features several modes familiar to TD veterans but adds its own unique spin. You can expect to encounter Story Mode, where you follow a narrative path; Infinite Mode, for testing your endurance; and Raids or Portals, which offer high-tier rewards for those who can overcome the toughest challenges. The progression system is deep, involving not just leveling up your units, but also applying traits and navigating complex evolution trees to unlock their true potential.

## Understanding Game Mechanics: Units, Traits, and Evolutions

To truly master the game without a **Manhwa Legends trello** to guide every click, you need to understand the underlying mechanics. Units in Manhwa Legends are not static; they can be improved through several different systems. The most basic is leveling, which increases base stats, but the real power comes from Traits and Evolutions.

Traits are random modifiers applied to a unit upon summoning or through a re-rolling mechanic. These can range from simple damage buffs to complex utility effects, such as increasing the range of nearby towers or slowing down enemies. Evolutions, however, are a more permanent form of progression. By gathering specific items—often found in Raids or Portals—you can evolve a unit into a much stronger version, often reflecting a character's "awakening" in their respective manhwa.

### Step-by-Step Evolution Process
1.  **Obtain the Base Unit:** Summon the unit you wish to evolve (usually Legendary or Mythical).
2.  **Gather Materials:** Clear specific Raid maps or Portals to collect evolution items.
3.  **Reach Max Level:** Ensure the base unit has reached the required level threshold.
4.  **Perform Evolution:** Use the evolution menu to consume materials and transform the unit.
5.  **Re-Equip Traits:** Note that some evolutions may allow for new, more powerful traits to be rolled.
