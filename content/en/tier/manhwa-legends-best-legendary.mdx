## S-Tier DPS: The Absolute Powerhouses

These units represent the current pinnacle of strength. According to community reports, these characters are mandatory for anyone serious about end-game content.

### Secret Dreamer (Constellation)
Currently, Secret Dreamer stands as the undisputed champion. Its full AOE damage paired with clone-based Shock stuns makes it a monster in wave-clear scenarios. 
*   **Unlock Path:** Invest in the Ancient (Regressor) skill tree to gather Constellations.
*   **Recommended Traits:** Legend or Limit Break.

### Song Hwen (Vessel)
While Secret Dreamer dominates AOE, Song Hwen is your go-to for single-target annihilation. If you are facing a boss with a massive health pool, this is the unit you want on your roster. Pair this unit with Boss Slayer traits for maximum efficiency.

### White Wolf (Feral)
White Wolf thrives on a compounding damage loop. By stacking Critical Chance until it reaches 100%, the unit triggers persistent Bleed effects that shred enemies. It is a reliable pick that holds up across every game mode.

## Essential Traits for Your Units

Traits are the final piece of the puzzle. They are assigned independently of base abilities and can drastically alter how a unit performs in the field. Always aim to reroll for these specific traits to maximize your damage output:

*   **Legend:** The "gold standard" for almost every unit. It offers a broad, consistent buff to your stats.
*   **Limit Break:** Best reserved for units that already have high base damage, as it pushes their ceiling even higher.
*   **Boss Slayer:** A non-negotiable trait for your primary boss killers.
*   **Assassin:** Ideal for crit-heavy units like White Wolf.
