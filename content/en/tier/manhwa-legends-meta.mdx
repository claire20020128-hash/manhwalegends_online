## Understanding the Current Farming Meta

Farming units are the backbone of any successful strategy in *Manhwa Legends*. Without a steady flow of income, you cannot upgrade your high-damage dealers during a match.

| Unit | Role | Rarity | Efficiency Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mint** | Farm | Rare | Accessible, great for early-game income. |
| **Lloyd** | Farm | Mythic | Higher yield, requires evolution for maximum efficiency. |

Community reports suggest that while Lloyd is significantly harder to obtain due to his mythic rarity, his potential for increased income through evolution makes him the superior choice for late-game setups. Mint remains a reliable, cost-effective starter, but players should aim to transition to Lloyd as soon as their roster allows.

## Top-Tier DPS and Boss Killers

Damage output is the primary metric for clearing end-game content. When analyzing the current state of the game, certain units stand out due to their unique passives and ability to scale damage throughout a match.

### S-Tier Powerhouses

*   **Dreamer:** A complex unit that utilizes clones to maximize damage. Unlocking him requires significant investment, including 1,800 constellations and a specific evolution item.
*   **Kim:** Highly versatile, Kim’s ability to copy the passives of other units makes him a staple in almost any high-damage lineup.
*   **Bomb:** An excellent mythic choice that offers a permanent damage buff and critical strike potential, making him a cornerstone of the meta.

### Boss-Killing Specialists

| Unit | Specialization | Key Advantage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sung Jinwoo's Dad** | Full AoE / Boss | Highest theoretical boss-killing potential. |
| **Chae-in** | Single-Target | Specialized kit designed purely for boss encounters. |
| **Banner Secret** | Summoning | Summons units based on damage dealt; scales with stacks. |

According to player experience, Sung Jinwoo's Dad is currently the gold standard for boss-killing. His full-area-of-effect damage allows him to clear waves while simultaneously chunking down boss health, providing unparalleled utility.

## Support and Utility Units

A team is only as strong as its support. In the current landscape, support units are no longer just about healing; they are about cleansing debuffs and providing critical damage buffs to your main DPS units.

*   **Idol:** An essential damage buffer that sends notes to friendly units, increasing their overall output.
*   **7-Day Login Unit:** Highly valued for her cleanse ability. By removing debuffs from friendly units, she effectively increases their survivability and damage uptime, which is vital for long-duration fights.

## Strategic Tips for Team Building

To maximize your efficiency in *Manhwa Legends*, consider these strategic approaches when building your team:

1.  **Synergy Over Raw Stats:** Always prioritize units that share damage types or passives. For example, units like Beast and Baylord gain significant bonuses when placed alongside other "Body" type units.
2.  **Focus on Evolution:** An evolved unit is almost always better than a high-level unit without evolution. Prioritize farming the materials needed for your core carry's evolution before leveling them to the cap.
3.  **Trait Rerolling:** Don't neglect your unit traits. A strong trait (like .1) can take a good unit and turn them into a meta-defining powerhouse.
4.  **Understand Your Goal:** Are you farming for currency or pushing the infinite leaderboard? Your team composition should shift based on the task. Keep a dedicated boss-killing unit like Chae-in ready for specific challenge stages.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most important unit in the current Manhwa Legends meta?
While it depends on your specific needs, Kim is widely considered one of the most valuable units because he can copy the passives of any other unit, making him extremely flexible in any composition.

### How do I effectively farm in Manhwa Legends?
To maximize income, you should prioritize Lloyd as your main farm unit. If you are just starting, use Mint until you have the resources to evolve and upgrade your Lloyd, as his late-game yield is significantly higher.

### Is it worth investing in the Banner Secret unit?
Yes, the Banner Secret unit is highly effective due to her summoning mechanic and stacking damage multipliers. She is particularly useful in scenarios where you need to manage multiple enemy targets simultaneously.
