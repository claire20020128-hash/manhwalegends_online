## Top-Tier Farming and Support Units

Farming units are the backbone of any successful run. According to community experience, while Lloyd is a Mythic unit that is harder to obtain, he generally offers a better long-term return on investment than Mint, especially once evolved.

For supports, your choice can drastically change your team’s survival rate. The 7-day login reward unit is a fantastic addition for players because she provides essential cleansing, which removes debuffs from your friendly units while simultaneously boosting their damage output.

## DPS Rankings: Who Should You Invest In?

When it comes to raw damage, the meta is currently shifting toward units that can either stack passives or copy the abilities of others. Kim remains a standout choice because of his unique ability to bookmark and mimic the passives of other units on your team. When paired with high-damage units like Bomb, Kim becomes a powerhouse that can carry you through challenging stages.

### S-Tier Damage Dealers

*   **Kim:** Versatile, copies passives, and provides consistent damage.
*   **Bomb:** Exceptional early-game unit, cheap to deploy, and gains massive permanent buffs once his "Four Nation" ability is unlocked.
*   **Sung Jinwoo's Dad:** Widely considered the premier boss killer due to his massive AoE potential and high damage ceiling.

Always remember that obtaining these units is only half the battle. Investing in the right traits—like the "Point One" trait—can be the difference between a unit that feels underwhelming and one that carries your team to victory. For more detailed game mechanics, check out the [official Roblox platform](https://www.roblox.com) to stay updated on patch notes and developer announcements.

## Units to Avoid (Bottom Tier)

Not every unit in the game is worth your resources. Some units, such as those exclusive to certain early-access periods or those with limited upgrade paths (like Pildo), often fail to keep up with the damage requirements of higher levels. 

For instance, certain units that rely on turret placement often struggle because their turrets cannot be buffed effectively and their overall DPS falls off significantly compared to modern meta units. Avoid sinking your hard-earned evolution items into these bottom-tier characters unless you are collecting them for fun.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which unit is best for beginners in this Manhwa Legends tier list?
For beginners, Bomb is arguably the best unit. He is relatively easy to obtain, very cheap to deploy early in a match, and provides significant permanent buffs that make progression much smoother.

### Is it worth upgrading every unit I summon?
No. Resources are finite, and you should prioritize units in the S and A tiers of this Manhwa Legends tier list. Focus your evolution items on your primary DPS and core support units before considering secondary characters.

### How do I get better units in the game?
You can obtain units through the banner system. Keep an eye out for "Banner Secret" units, which are typically stronger than standard pool units, though they are harder to pull. Always check the community forums to see if a new banner unit is worth your gems before spending.

### Why is Kim considered top tier?
Kim's strength lies in his flexibility. Because he can copy the passives of any other unit on your team, he effectively adapts to whatever role you need most, making him a staple in almost every high-level team composition.
