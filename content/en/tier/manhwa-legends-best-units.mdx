## S-Tier: The Absolute Best Units in Manhwa Legends

If you are lucky enough to pull these units, they should immediately become the focal point of your team. These characters represent the pinnacle of damage and are essential for anyone looking to top the leaderboards in 2026.

### Secret Dreamer
Secret Dreamer is widely considered the king of the current meta. What sets this unit apart is its dual-threat capability. While many units excel at either bossing or wave clearing, Secret Dreamer handles both with ease. Community reports highlight its "Dream Scaling" mechanic, which allows it to gain massive damage buffs as the match progresses, making it virtually untouchable in the late game.

### Song Hwan
Consistency is the name of the game for Song Hwan. In high-stakes raids where one missed hit can lead to a wipe, Song Hwan’s high attack speed and reliable hitboxes make him a favorite for hardcore players. He doesn't rely on RNG or perfect conditions; he simply outputs massive damage from the moment he is placed until the final boss falls.

### White Wolf
White Wolf is the go-to choice for players who prioritize burst potential. In the 2026 meta, certain bosses have "enrage" timers that require you to dump millions of damage in a matter of seconds. White Wolf’s ability to stack critical hits makes him the premier choice for overcoming these gear checks.

### Beast and Baylord
Beast offers raw, unadulterated offensive power. He is often used in Infinite Mode to thin out the herd before they reach the base. Baylord, on the other hand, is the ultimate "complete package" unit, offering a blend of range, speed, and scaling that makes him viable in literally every game mode available.

## A-Tier: High-Performance Alternatives

Not everyone will pull an S-tier unit on their first day. Fortunately, the A-tier consists of units that are nearly as powerful and can comfortably carry you through the end-game content. These are the **Manhwa Legends best units** for players who want a competitive edge without the extreme rarity of the S-tier.

### Hayori and Cherry
Hayori is often cited as the most reliable unit for story mode progression. Her scaling is smooth, meaning she never feels weak at any point in the match. Cherry, conversely, is a wave-clearing specialist. If you find yourself struggling with large groups of enemies leaking past your defenses, Cherry’s area-of-effect (AoE) capabilities are a lifesaver.

### Ancient and Karasha
Ancient is perhaps the most balanced unit in the game. He doesn't have a glaring weakness, making him a "safe" pick for blind runs of new content. Karasha is favored for her sustained damage in long-form boss encounters, where she can outpace even some S-tier units if the fight lasts long enough.

## B-Tier and C-Tier: The Progression Path

Units in these tiers are not "bad," but they are eventually outclassed. Understanding when to pivot away from these characters is key to efficient resource management.

### The B-Tier Mid-Game Staples
Units like **Kim Dokja** and **Infernum** are fantastic for the middle portion of your journey. Kim Dokja, in particular, is often called the "gatekeeper" of the high tiers. He provides enough damage to get you through most raids, but you will start to notice his limitations once you hit the ultra-difficult 2026 end-game stages. **Yu**, **Jay**, and **Koaera de Noir** also fall into this category—dependable, but not meta-defining.

### The C-Tier: Early Game Only
**Pildo Gang** and **Creator** are your starting blocks. They are designed to help you understand the game's mechanics and clear the initial chapters. ## How to Maximize Damage with Your Units

Simply owning the **Manhwa Legends best units** isn't enough; you must know how to use them. The 2026 meta emphasizes "placement synergy" and "upgrade priority."

1.  **Prioritize Your Carry:** In any given match, identify which of your units has the best scaling (usually your S-tier). Focus all your initial upgrades on them rather than spreading your gold across five different units.
2.  **Range Overlap:** Ensure your units' ranges overlap at "kill zones"—specific parts of the map where enemies are slowed or grouped together.
3.  **Monitor Attack Speed:** Some units benefit more from speed buffs than raw damage buffs. Song Hwan, for example, becomes a monster when paired with speed-enhancing supports.
4.  **Infinite Mode Strategy:** For Infinite Mode, you need a balance of AoE (Cherry/Beast) and Single Target (White Wolf/Song Hwan). If you go all-in on one, you will eventually leak enemies.

As you move into the highest level of play, the **Manhwa Legends best units** require specific tactical approaches. Community reports from top-tier guilds suggest that "staggered placement" is now the preferred method for tackling bosses with high health pools. By placing your high-burst units like White Wolf at the start of a path and sustained damage units like Karasha in the middle, you ensure that the boss is constantly under pressure.

Furthermore, pay attention to the elemental or type advantages that may be introduced in seasonal updates. While the core rankings of the **Manhwa Legends best units** remain stable, a specific boss might have a resistance that makes a B-tier unit temporarily more viable than an S-tier one. Always stay adaptable.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which of the Manhwa Legends best units is easiest for beginners to use?
For beginners, **Hayori** is the best choice. She has very straightforward mechanics, smooth upgrade scaling, and enough range to cover for poor placement. While not as powerful as Secret Dreamer, she is much more forgiving for players still learning the ropes of the 2026 meta.

### Is Kim Dokja still worth using in the late game?
Kim Dokja is a solid B-tier unit, but he generally falls off in the very late game. While he can participate in raids, he lacks the explosive scaling found in the S-tier units. If he is your only high-rarity unit, use him, but look to replace him with someone like **Song Hwan** or **Baylord** as soon as possible.

### How often does the Manhwa Legends meta change?
The meta typically shifts with every major content update, which usually happens every few months. However, the **Manhwa Legends best units** like Secret Dreamer and Song Hwan have remained at the top due to their fundamental design and scaling mechanics. It is always wise to keep an eye on patch notes for any significant balance changes.

### Should I focus on one S-tier unit or a full team of A-tier units?
In most PvE scenarios, one highly-leveled S-tier unit like **Secret Dreamer** will outperform a full team of lower-level A-tier units. The game's mechanics heavily reward deep investment into a single "carry" unit that can handle the bulk of the damage.
